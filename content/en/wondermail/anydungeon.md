##### Before You Begin

- Advance the story to the point where Crevice Cave is cleared (Secret Rank).
- Clear Special Episode 1, Bidoof's Wish, once.

##### Steps

1. Select the region and the dungeon you want to unlock, then generate a password.
2. Enter the generated password in Wonder Mail S.
3. When you execute the mission, a message will appear saying that Star Cave has been unlocked, but the selected dungeon will actually be unlocked as well.

- You may delete the mission after unlocking it.

##### Notes

- If Star Cave has not yet been unlocked, both Star Cave and the selected dungeon will be unlocked.
- Once a dungeon is unlocked, missions will start appearing on the Job Board and Outlaw Board.
- Hidden Land and Temporal Tower cannot be entered even after unlocking them. (They can be entered from Deep/Spire.)
- Missions for Hidden Land and Temporal Tower can be accepted by entering through Lapras.
- The Nightmare cannot be entered even after unlocking it.
- If you unlock Sealed Ruin and proceed all the way to the end, it will count as clearing the dungeon without advancing to Deep.
- Even if The Nightmare or Destiny Tower is unlocked once, you cannot accept missions for that dungeon (and no missions will appear).
- No missions will appear on the Job Board or Outlaw Board for subsequent dungeons.
- If you unlock a boss-floor dungeon and defeat the boss, the recruit event will occur again.
- Unlocking and entering a Dummy (0xAD) will not work on real hardware and will cause the game to crash.
- Demo dungeons generally cannot be entered, but you can enter them by setting your Poké to 0.
- <span class="text-danger">Unlocking and clearing a story dungeon that is later than your current progress may affect story progression.</span>
- <span class="text-danger">Be especially careful, since saving will make it impossible to return to the original state.</span>
