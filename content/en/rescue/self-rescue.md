A self-rescue tool for Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time, Explorers of Darkness, and Explorers of Sky.
Supports all game versions and regions.

##### Steps

Simply enter the SOS Mail password and rescue is completed instantly. You can generate the corresponding A-OK Mail and receive the items as well.

**No waiting for rescue, and no human kindness!**

##### By the way

- By entering an A-OK Mail password, you can generate a Thank-You Mail. This is recommended for getting rid of any leftover Mail.
- It can also be sent to dungeons where items normally cannot be delivered (Zero Isle West, South).
